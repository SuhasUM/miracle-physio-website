Advanced Physiotherapy Services

Modern, targeted treatment approaches designed to support pain relief, mobility, performance, and recovery.

Specialised Care

Advanced Treatment for Better Recovery

At Miracle Advance Physiotherapy, every treatment plan is selected according to your condition, comfort, and recovery goals under the guidance of Dr B S Rajput.

Pain Management

Laser Therapy

A non-invasive treatment approach that may help support pain relief, tissue healing, and recovery from musculoskeletal conditions.

Muscle Recovery

Dry Needling Therapy

A targeted technique used to address tight muscle areas and trigger points, helping improve movement and reduce muscle-related discomfort.

Soft Tissue Care

Cupping Therapy

A supportive therapy technique that can help ease muscle tightness, improve soft-tissue mobility, and complement rehabilitation plans.

Movement Support

Kinesio Taping

Flexible therapeutic tape applied to support muscles and joints while allowing natural movement during daily activities and rehabilitation.

Active Recovery

Sports Injury Management

Assessment and rehabilitation for sports-related pain and injuries, with a focus on restoring strength, mobility, and return to activity.

Rehabilitation

Ortho & Neuro Rehab

Personalised rehabilitation for orthopaedic and neurological conditions, supporting functional movement, balance, strength, and independence.

Soft Tissue Mobilisation

IASTM Therapy

Instrument-assisted soft tissue mobilisation is used to address restricted soft tissues and support better mobility as part of a guided treatment plan.

Child Development

Paediatric Neuro Therapy

Supportive physiotherapy for children with developmental and neurological needs, focused on mobility, balance, coordination, and functional skills.

Communication Support

Speech Therapy

Personalised support to improve communication, speech clarity, language skills, and confidence for children and adults with speech and language difficulties.

Functional Independence

Occupational Therapy

Goal-focused therapy that supports independence in daily activities, fine motor skills, sensory needs, self-care, learning, and functional participation.

Women's Wellness

Women's Health Physiotherapy

Individualised care for pregnancy-related pain, antenatal exercise, postnatal recovery, pelvic floor rehabilitation, core strengthening, and safe return to activity.

Personalised Treatment Matters
