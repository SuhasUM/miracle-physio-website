About Our Clinic

Where Expertise Meets Compassionate Care

Miracle Advance Physiotherapy Clinic is a patient-focused rehabilitation centre in Banashankari 6th Stage, Bengaluru. We help people move better, recover with confidence, and return to the activities they love.

Led by Dr B S Rajput, our clinic combines clinical experience, modern rehabilitation equipment, and personalised care to support every patient’s unique recovery journey.
